The Underwater Waterfall of Mauritius

By GLOBAL INFO Staff

Off the coast of Mauritius lies one of the world's most amazing natural illusions: the underwater waterfall.

From above, it appears as though water is plunging into a giant underwater abyss. In reality, the effect is created by sand and sediment moving along underwater slopes.

The illusion has attracted tourists, photographers and nature lovers from around the globe.

Today, it remains one of the most fascinating sights in the Indian Ocean.
