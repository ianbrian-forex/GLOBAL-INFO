⚡ The Place Where Lightning Never Stops

By GLOBAL INFO Staff

Published: September 18, 2026

In Venezuela, near Lake Maracaibo, a remarkable natural phenomenon lights up the sky for many nights each year.

Known as Catatumbo Lightning, the area experiences frequent thunderstorms that can produce thousands of lightning flashes.

Scientists believe local geography, warm air, moisture, and wind patterns help create ideal conditions for these storms.

The spectacular display has earned a reputation as one of the most active lightning regions on Earth.
